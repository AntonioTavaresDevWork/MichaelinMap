## What the material delivers

A guide to places curated by a single critic ("Michael"), public and read-only, global in scope but 91%
Austin. The moat is the judgment: a scarce tier (destination › experience › fair for restaurants, cool ›
fair for bars), a star crossing the tiers (22/511 = 4%), tags describing purpose and not just category, and
a personality layer (Codes that transform the UI at runtime, absurdist field reports). The schema is ready,
the seed has 93 tags + 38 questions, the CSV is clean, and the plan has 9 phases with an exit test each.

Good material. I validated the PRD's numbers against the CSV and they all match (511 rows, 273 restaurants,
91 bars, 22 starred, 42 unvisited, 28 TIER+UNVISITED conflicts, 19 source guides, 0 duplicate Apple IDs, 0
missing coordinates).

## Findings that change the plan

Seven things I found that are not in the documents:

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The 511 places arrive with no tags.** The CSV's `Tags` column has only 5 distinct values (Breakfast & brunch 54, Rooftop 14, Night out 3, Vacation 2, Food truck 1). The 93-tag vocabulary is born 100% empty | Bottleneck #1 of the project. Faceted filtering is Phase 2 and the heart of the product — and it has no data. Somebody has to tag 511 places by hand |
| 2 | **`codes` leaks every code.** The policy `public reads active codes using (active = true)` lets any visitor run `select * from codes` and list every secret code | Kills the feature the PRD §4.1 calls the most important one |
| 3 | **`field_reports` accepts free inserts.** `with check (true)` lets a visitor write `status='published'` directly, bypassing the review gate on the 4 free-text questions | Violates hard constraint #8 and the "zero unreviewed free text published" goal |
| 4 | **Any authenticated account is a curator.** `auth.role() = 'authenticated'` + open signup in Supabase = a stranger creates an account and writes to the guide | A real security failure |
| 5 | **`import-places.ts` breaks at runtime.** It does `upsert(onConflict: 'apple_id')` but `apple_id` has no UNIQUE in the schema | Phase 0 does not pass |
| 6 | **The seed is not idempotent.** `questions` has no uniqueness constraint — running it twice duplicates the 38 questions | PLAN requires idempotency at the Phase 0 exit |
| 7 | **4 places violate the tier × type crossing:** Grocery with Fair (2) and Destination (1), Bar with Experience (1), Outdoors with Fair (1) | If I enforce the rule by constraint (which is the right thing), those 4 rows are rejected |

Smaller, but recorded: the `field_report_aggregates` view needs `security_invoker = on` or it bypasses RLS;
`Hype trap` is cited in PRD §6.4 but does not exist among the seed's 93 tags; PRD §9.10 describes a
`Collection` primitive unifying codes, lists and a shortlist that does not exist in the schema; and
`place_tags` has an unrestricted public SELECT (leaking the ids of unpublished places).

## Where the material collides with this project's rules

`.claude/CLAUDE.md` (the Wise* framework) and the CLAUDE.md that came from Claude Web contradict each other
in four places. My recommendations — I just need your OK:

| Wise* rule | Michaelin Map | I recommend |
|---|---|---|
| `company_id` + multi-tenant RLS on every table | A single curator, a public product | Drop multi-tenancy, record it as an ADR in the bible. Forcing `company_id` here is ceremony without function |
| UI in Portuguese BR, format `1.000,00`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `R$` | The whole product in English, Austin, `$`/`$$$` | UI and content in English, en-US format. Code comments stay in English (already the rule). Our conversation stays in PT-BR |
| Soft delete through `deleted_at` | `status = closed \| hidden` | Accept `status` as the mechanism — it is more expressive here |
| Mutation through a `SECURITY DEFINER` RPC | Direct writes from the client | Keep the rule: `rpc_submit_field_report` and `rpc_redeem_code` solve findings #2 and #3 at once |

And two new dependencies need your explicit approval (the "never install a new dependency without an OK"
rule): **MapLibre GL** (mandatory — Codes depend on swapping the map style at runtime, which a Google embed
cannot do) and the **Google Places API** (hydrating hours, phone numbers and price band — about 511
one-shot requests, estimated cost under US$10).

## My questions — what I need from you

On the business and the process:

1. **Who is the operational curator?** The product speaks of "Michael" (Michael's Top Faves) but you are
   the dev. Is Michael a client, a partner, or is this your project with a persona? That defines who
   operates the admin, who answers the 7 open questions in PRD §16, and who will tag the 511 places.
2. **Who tags?** Finding #1 is the critical path. Without tags the product is a pretty map of pins. It is
   511 places × ~5 facets. My recommendation: use the Google Places API types to pre-populate `cuisine` and
   `format` automatically during hydration (Phase 4), leaving the curator only to review and add the
   subjective facets — occasion, vibe, character. That turns weeks into hours. Are you up for that
   approach, or does the curator want to assign everything by hand on principle?
3. **What is the deadline and the ambition?** Nine phases is a large MVP. If there is a demo date, I cut
   scope to reach it — PRD §4.1 makes clear that success is "showing it to one specific person", which
   suggests Phases 0-3 plus 7 (Codes) deliver the value and Phases 5, 6, 8 and 9 can wait.

On execution:

4. **Import: script or migration?** `import-places.ts` needs the SERVICE_ROLE_KEY on your machine. I
   recommend I convert the CSV into an idempotent seed migration applied through MCP — it stays versioned,
   does not expose the service role, and solves findings #5 and #6 together. Confirm?
5. **The 5 curation questions in the PRD that block phases and that nobody but the curator can answer:**
   (a) is `destination` really above `experience`? (b) cities with a single place — show them as pairs,
   group the Texan ones under "Central Texas", or suppress them? (c) do the public tier names stay as they
   are, or does he rename them? (d) is `Hype trap` public or admin-only? (e) is the link indexable by
   Google, or unlisted? — Can you get those answers, or should I work with defaults and record them as debt
   in the BACKLOG?
